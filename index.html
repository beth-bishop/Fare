How to play
  • Roll all 6 dice. Every roll, set aside at least one scoring die.
  • Push it or stop. Roll what's left to build your turn, or complete your turn to keep the points.
  • Farkle. No scoring dice on a roll? You lose everything from that turn.
  • Hot dice. Score with all 6 and you get to roll all 6 again.
  • Get on the board. Your first turn needs at least 500 to count.
  • Three Farkles in a row costs you 1,000.
  • Final round. Hit the winning score (10,000) and everyone else gets one last turn to beat you.
